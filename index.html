To the girl I found by chance   ♥

Found by Chance

I found you where I wasn't looking,

just a name beneath a "suggested" screen,

one little request, one quiet accept—

and somehow, you became a thought in between.

I don't know your favorite song,

or the stories you keep untold,

I barely know the person behind the pictures,

yet talking to you never felt old.

And I call you D,

though I know nobody else does.

Maybe that's my little way

of making something ordinary feel like ours.

You're cute—

I won't pretend I didn't notice.

But it wasn't only the way you looked;

there was something in talking to you

that made me want to stay a little longer.

Then I asked,

"Am I disturbing you?"

You said,

"Little bit."

So I said goodbye.

Not because I wanted to,

but because sometimes

liking a conversation

also means knowing when to leave it.

So here I am,

not asking you for anything,

not expecting you to come back—

just leaving these few words here,

for the girl I met by accident,

the girl I barely know,

but somehow still think about

a little more than I probably should.

♥

— Stranger

for you, always in this moment
