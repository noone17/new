For My Baby Girl   Nikita   ♥

Two Days, Somehow

Two days ago,

you were just a name on my screen,

Nikita — a stranger, somewhere in between.

And somehow, in these little conversations we share,

you've become a thought I find everywhere.

You call me beloved,

and somehow that name sounds sweeter from you.

So I'm learning Hindi, little by little,

just to understand the world you speak into.

I love that shy little laugh of yours,

the way you blush when our words get close.

You try to hide it, but I notice it all—

the smile you pretend nobody knows.

My baby girl,

I don't want to rush whatever this becomes.

I just want to know you, slowly,

one conversation, one smile, one moment at a time.

I like your eyes most—

because they say things your words don't.

And your smile…

God, I could get lost there without knowing I'm gone.

Maybe someday, when we're sitting close,

I'll kiss your forehead gently,

then your nose,

and if you let me, your lips—

not because I want to steal a moment,

but because I want to remember it.

I'd kiss the little moles

that make you unmistakably you,

and hold your waist a little closer

like the world could wait for us.

And maybe I'll kiss your feet too,

not because they're beautiful alone,

but because every part of you

deserves to be cherished.

I know it's only been two days.

Maybe that's too soon for forever.

So I won't promise you forever.

I'll just promise this—

while you're here,

I'll be here too.

Learning your Hindi.

Learning your silence.

Learning your laughter.

Learning the girl behind the screen.

Because, Nikita,

I don't just want to look at you.

I want to know you.

I want to be with you in the moment.

And maybe, someday,

become one of the reasons you blush.

♥

— beloved

for you, always in this moment
