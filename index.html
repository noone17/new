A little note from the heart

For you, Shakthi

The world was ordinary until you came,
A little Instagram chat, a beautiful name.
Just one day of talking, yet somehow I knew,
There was something different about you.

And then I saw you in that Onam white,
Like a little piece of heaven dressed in light.
That saree, that smile, that beautiful view,
For a moment, the world forgot everything but you.

Those rose-petal lips, that playful glow,
Those bubbly cheeks that steal the show.
You looked so lovely, so effortlessly sweet,
Like a dream my heart never expected to meet.

I know it's only been a day
And I don't know everything about you.
I haven't heard all the stories you hold,
Or the little secrets your heart hasn't told.

But sometimes, feelings don't wait for time,
They arrive like a song, like a beautiful rhyme.
And somewhere between your laughter and smile,
You made my ordinary day worthwhile.

So I asked you a question, a little afraid,
Hoping my feelings wouldn't leave you dismayed.
“Shakthi, will you be my girl?” I said,
With a thousand little butterflies inside my head.

And if you say yes, I'll smile at my phone,
No longer a stranger, no longer alone.
But even if your heart takes its own sweet time,
I'll be glad I met you, my unexpected rhyme.

Because before I knew you, you were just a name,
Now even that name doesn't feel quite the same.
Shakthi, my pretty little Onam dream,
You've become the sweetest thought in my daydream. 🤍🌸
