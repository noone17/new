They say the moon borrows its light,
But yours feels like it was born to shine.

Maybe that is why they call you Luna—
Because even a quiet glance from you
Can make an ordinary day feel brighter.

I do not know your favorite song,
Or the stories you have never told.

I only know that when I saw you,
Something about your smile
Made me stop scrolling.

Your eyes carry a calm I cannot explain,
As if they hold a thousand gentle thoughts.

Your glasses do not hide their beauty—
They frame them,
Like a perfect poem in a simple border.

Your cheeks wear kindness so naturally,
And your smile feels effortless,
The kind that lingers in someone's mind
Long after the screen goes dark.

Maybe this is just the beginning,
A hello between two strangers.

Maybe it is nothing more than a passing moment.
But I would rather be honest than silent.

Since the day I found you,
I have caught myself smiling for no reason,
Thinking about someone
I barely know,
Yet somehow wish to know deeply.

It may sound unexpected,
And perhaps even a little crazy,
But feelings do not always wait for permission.

So here is my heart,
Simple, honest, and without pretense:

Luna, I think I have fallen for you.

I do not know where this journey will lead,
But I know I would love the chance
To walk the first few steps with you.

If love begins with admiration,
Grows through respect,
And blossoms through knowing someone,
Then I am ready for every chapter.

Hasin Samina,
You are more than a beautiful face to me.

You are the reason I paused,
The reason I hoped,
And the reason I am brave enough to say this—

I love you.
Not because I know everything about you,
But because my heart believes
You are someone truly worth knowing,
Cherishing, and loving.

Whether you feel the same or not,
Thank you for being the reason
My heart learned to write poems again.

With all my heart,
Yours truly
