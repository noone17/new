To Her Majesty, Queen Leshna,
It began with a glance on Aisle, a moment passing by, Then curiosity got the better of me, so I went looking for your smile. I found you on Instagram and took a chance to say hello, Not knowing that a simple message could make me want to know you more. You're beautiful, yes — that's the easiest thing to see, But your kindness in our conversations is what means more to me. Between your books, your dreams, and every busy day, I'd love to be a little reason you smile along the way. Maybe we could start with a movie and popcorn to share, Then let the evening unfold with music in the air. A moonlit walk along the beach, with the waves beside our feet, I'd love to hold your hand as we walk, making that moment feel complete. Perhaps my arm gently around your waist, if you're comfortable too, Just enjoying the sea breeze, the stars, and a little time with you. I might flirt enough to make you blush, then pretend I didn't see, And secretly hope that smile is meant just for me. Maybe we'll laugh at silly things, talk about dreams till late, And make a beautiful memory from our very first date. No pressure, no grand promises, no script for how things start, Just one lovely evening and a chance to know your heart. So, Leshna, here's the question I've been wanting to ask… Would you let me take you on a date? ❤️
With admiration and a hopeful heart,
Your humble admirer
