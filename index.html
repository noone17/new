Somehow, It Was You

I saw you on Instagram, completely by chance,
Just another face in a world full of endless scrolling.
I didn't know your name, your story, or your life,
I wasn't even looking for someone that night.
But somehow, I stopped when I saw you there,
Maybe it was your smile, maybe something in your eyes.
I can't explain what made me stay,
I just know I didn't scroll away.
So I sent a simple message, nothing too clever,
Just "what are you doing?"—that's all I had.
I thought maybe we'd talk for a little while,
Never imagined I'd remember every reply.
Then came your "why should I tell you?"
And honestly, I didn't know what to say. 😂
You had this little attitude about you,
And somehow, I found it strangely cute.
I said, "You don't have to, I was just curious,"
Trying to play it cool, pretending I didn't care.
Then I asked if I could know something about you,
And you simply said, "No." 😒
You weren't in the mood to tell me anything,
And I probably should've just disappeared.
But there was something about the way you talked,
That made a random stranger suddenly feel interesting.
Then somehow we started talking about weather,
And even that became a little argument. 😂
You laughed, I wondered why,
You said I was being sarcastic—
And somewhere between all those silly replies,
I realized I was enjoying this conversation.
Maybe that's what makes some people different,
You don't need a perfect first conversation.
Sometimes it's the awkward replies,
The teasing, the sarcasm, the unexpected moments,
That make you want to stay a little longer.
I found you randomly on Instagram,
With no plan, no expectations, nothing in mind.
But now, when I think about that random scroll,
I wonder how one little moment changed my night.
Maybe it was just an algorithm,
Maybe it was simply coincidence.
But if you ask me what I think about it now,
I'd say I'm glad Instagram made that mistake—or maybe it didn't.
Because among thousands of faces I could have seen,
Somehow, it showed me yours.
And I still don't know where this conversation will go,
Or whether you'll laugh at this poem too.
But one thing I know for sure—
That random moment wasn't completely random to me.
Because I came to Instagram just to scroll,
And somehow, I ended up finding you. ❤️

Nikita ✦

I'm glad I didn't scroll past.
