I saw you once through a little screen,

but somehow you stayed in my thoughts unseen.

Your dusky glow, your soft, chubby charm,

there's something about you that feels so warm.

Maybe it's your smile, maybe it's your eyes,

or the way you carry your beautiful vibe.

I call you my brownie, sweet and rare,

the kind of sweetness I'd love to share. ❤️

And if I ever get the chance to take you on that beach date,

I don't think I'll be looking at the sunset much…

I'd probably be too busy looking at you. 🌅❤️

I'd want to walk beside you slowly,

let our hands accidentally meet…

then maybe hold your hand properly,

and keep walking while the waves touch our feet.

And if you ask me what I like about you…

Your colour.

Your softness.

Your smile.

Your whole vibe.

But there's one thing I really want to experience in person…

Your talkative side. 😌

Because texting you is one thing,

but I want to sit beside you someday,

listen to you talk about everything and nothing,

watch your expressions while you tell your stories,

and secretly smile because…

you're even more adorable when you're being talkative. ❤️

So Rose, maybe someday

let me take you for that beach walk…

and you can do all the talking.

I'll just hold your hand, listen to you,

and enjoy having you beside me. 🌊🌹
