Fatima,

I met you in an aisle,

and somehow a simple hello

turned into days of talking.

Maybe it's your smile,

maybe the way you make me laugh,

or maybe it's just how easy

it feels to talk to you.

There's something beautiful

about the traditional woman I see in you,

especially when you showed me

that Onam saree.

I honestly didn't know

a picture could leave me that speechless.

Beautiful feathers suit a beautiful bird,

and somehow those colours just made sense on you.

Not because the saree makes you beautiful,

but because you made the saree

look beautiful too.

Maybe one night,

when the city finally goes quiet,

I'd like to take you to the beach.

Just you and me,

the moon above us,

the waves beside us,

and your hand in mine.

I'd hold your hand a little longer,

walk a little slower,

maybe let our fingers intertwine

and forget about everything else for a while.

No rush, no plans—

just walking beside you under the moonlight,

talking about random things,

laughing at things that aren't even funny,

and secretly wishing

the walk would never end.

For now, I just know…

I met a girl named

Fatima

with a beautiful smile,

a wicked sense of humour,

a guarded heart…

"and feathers that belong

to a beautiful bird." 🪶
