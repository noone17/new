A moment I keep replaying

For you, Shorty

My Shorty, my Tiny Devil,
My little Short Hitler,
So nice, so beautiful, so hot,
So strong, with extra sugar.

Today you came in a pink saree,
Single pleat, with a gold bangle on your hand,
Rose-colored nails on your hands,
And beauty beyond compare.

We’ve talked in messages and calls,
I’ve heard your beautiful voice,
I’ve heard you sing so nicely,
And still, I had no choice.

When I met you today,
I just smiled through it all,
I don’t know why I love listening
To your blabbering.

In that saree, you looked gorgeous,
I just said, “You look good,”
But inside, I had so much more.

You looked so beautiful today,
More than my words could ever say,
I wish I told you everything
I felt when I saw you that way.

Today, you gave me a hug.
It was a little moment,
But it made my whole day.

I hope we can meet again soon —
And next time, maybe I can have one
That lasts a little longer.

One little moment, all day long

That hug meant everything.

I didn’t find all the right words today. So I saved one more little thing for you.

Next time, maybe I can have one that lasts a little longer.
Just you and me, close.

Until then, I’m keeping today’s hug with me. ❤️

Made with a full heart ♥
