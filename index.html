Farhana

🌙❤️

If the world ever feels a little too heavy…

Farhana, if I could borrow one evening with you,

I'd take you to the beach, beneath the sky so blue.

We'd walk along the shore as the sun fades away,

with a thousand little things we'd have to say.

I'd love to hold your hand as the ocean breeze blows,

walk beside you where the golden sunset glows.

And if you felt comfortable, I'd hold you by the waist,

making memories together, with no need to haste.

So, Farhana,

will you go on a beach date with me someday? 🌊

Just a sunset, a walk, and a little time away.

I'll try my best to make you smile and brighten up your day,

and if you'd like that too, we'll let the waves lead the way. ❤️

No pressure, no expectations —

just an invitation from me to you.
