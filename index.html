For you, Sahithi

From another state, you walked into my story,
And somehow made ordinary moments feel like poetry.

And hearing you missed me made me smile foolishly.

You're so tiny, yet you make my heartbeat race,
One hug from you, and I forget every other place.
But seeing you in that Diwali saree tonight,
Damn, Sahithi, you made my heart fall at first sight. ❤️

I wouldn't waste a second turning this into a page.
I'd look straight into your eyes, hold your hand, and say,

"Sahithi, will you be mine?" — I'd propose without delay. 💍❤️

My heart is already having a hard time behaving! 😉
