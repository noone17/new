To My Lady  🌸

🌸 🌸 🌸

The Girl in the Pink Saree

A poem written from heart, to yours.

💗 💗 💗

I saw you in an aisle, a stranger passing by,

Never thought you'd be the one who'd stay on my mind.

Found you on Instagram, and we started to speak,

Just one day of talking, yet there's something unique.

🌸

I like the way you talk, so bold and so fierce,

A beautiful soul with a presence that captivates.

That pink saree, white blouse — you looked so divine,

That little nose pin? Girl, you should be a crime. 😉

Your neatly styled hair, that gorgeous face,

How am I supposed to forget you with such effortless grace? ❤️

🌸

Maybe it's too soon, maybe it's just a spark,

But somehow, you've left a little glow in my heart.

I barely know you, yet I'd love to know more,

Meet you in person, not just through a screen anymore.

🌸

Maybe someday, beneath the evening sky,

We'll walk hand in hand, just you and I.

No promises, no pressure, just a moment to see,

If the girl in the pink saree might smile walking beside me. ❤️

🌸

"Can I hold your hand and take a walk with you someday?"

No rush, no pressure — just two people, an evening sky, and a quiet possibility.

— With quiet admiration 💗

Written for you, the girl in the pink saree 🌸
